2D airfoil · externalXFOILPanel method with viscous boundary-layer couplingEstablished reference

Application. Fast 2D airfoil coefficients, pressure distributions, transition estimates, and polars in attached or moderately separated flow. Requires the optional executable.

Mathematical method. Inviscid potential-flow panels are coupled to integral laminar and turbulent boundary-layer and wake equations, including transition and viscous interaction. It is not a three-dimensional Navier–Stokes simulation.

Maturity. Established external solver and a useful comparison baseline. Strong separation, dynamic stall, three-dimensional effects, and geometry outside its airfoil model need different evidence.

Workspace. Airfoil Lab backend xfoil; a local executable is optional and is not the JXfoil source.

2D airfoil · JuliaJXfoilPanel solve with coupled viscous and wake equationsCross-checked

Application. Quick airfoil coefficients, surface pressure, transition, boundary-layer state, and polar sweeps. It is the in-workspace alternative to XFOIL for the same class of 2D section problems.

Mathematical method. Node-based linear-vorticity potential-flow panels feed a coupled boundary-layer and wake Newton solve. Displacement thickness changes the inviscid edge flow; skin friction and wake momentum thickness contribute to drag.

Maturity. The strongest parity evidence among the in-house paths: bundled inviscid and viscous suites report close agreement with XFOIL. Agreement with XFOIL does not validate separated stall or 3D flow against experiments.

Workspace. third_party/JXfoil/; Airfoil Lab backend jxfoil.

2D airfoil · CPU/GPUPressure-based low-speed CFDIncompressible finite-volume flow with optional SST turbulenceWorking beta

Application. Viscous low-Mach airfoil sections and steady polars where a resolved flow field is needed. The current Airfoil Lab mode uses the low-speed, single-phase part of PBCav.

Mathematical method. Collocated finite-volume momentum equations, Rhie–Chow face fluxes, pressure correction, and PIMPLE-style coupling enforce incompressible continuity. The working airfoil configuration includes k–ω SST turbulence and a C-mesh.

Maturity. Working development backend. Saved NACA 0012 cases reached the solver's steady residual stop and have pre-stall comparisons; mesh quality, grid independence, and high-incidence/stall behavior remain under study. A stable lift or drag trace alone is not a convergence certificate.

Workspace. solvers/pbcav_s1030/; Airfoil Lab backend lowspeed. Its real-water phase-change extension below is incomplete.

2D airfoil · CPU/GPUCompressible C-mesh CFDConservative compressible finite-volume equationsResearch backend

Application. Compressible airfoil flow, including transonic pressure recovery and shocks, with resolved surface and field outputs.

Mathematical method. A body-fitted C-mesh carries conservative mass, momentum, and energy updates. The active KernelAbstractions CPU/GPU carrier shares a rotated-face kinetic flux-vector-splitting core with the older CPU implementation; viscous stresses and turbulence models are coupled to it.

Maturity. Experimental CFD. A saved RAE 2822 Case 9 comparison placed loads and the main pressure-rise bracket near reference data, but its continuity guard reported stalled and neither equation nor grid convergence was established. Inspect residuals, mass balance, and mesh before interpreting a result.

Workspace. src/compressible/ and shared src/cmesh/; Airfoil Lab backend transonic.

2D hydrofoil · CPU/GPUConservative Species cavitationMixture flow with transported vapour mass and phase-change sourceResearch · partial validation

Application. Cavitating hydrofoils and water-tunnel cases for research comparisons. The implementation calls its current stepping module “production” to distinguish it from the legacy TEM step; that label does not mean predictive accuracy is established.

Mathematical method. The C-mesh carrier advances conservative mixture mass and momentum. A separate conservative vapour partial-density equation transports composition; an equation of state and evaporation/condensation source couple phase and pressure. The current path has GPU execution and restart machinery.

Maturity. Runnable but not validated for general cavitation prediction. In the recorded reference campaign one case partly matched loads and cavity length, while a held-out higher-cavitation case failed cavity length and pressure shape. That campaign was paused.

Workspace. src/cavitation/cmesh_cav_species.jl, shared src/cmesh/, and tools/cmesh_cav_movie.jl; Airfoil Lab backend cavitation.

3D geometry · GPU/AMRLUDWIG D3Q27 + cumulantLow-Mach lattice Boltzmann flowDevelopment

Application. Three-dimensional low-Mach external flow around imported geometry. Aero Lab allows geometry placement, mesh inspection, and adaptive refinement; Airfoil Lab uses a spanwise-periodic extruded section.

Mathematical method. A 27-velocity lattice Boltzmann population streams and collides using a cumulant operator. Block-structured AMR refines the obstacle and wake; macroscopic density, velocity, and surface outputs are reconstructed from the populations.

Maturity. Development solver with runnable GPU paths and mesh/diagnostic tools. Airfoil predictions and arbitrary-body force accuracy are not yet validated. Check wall resolution, AMR interfaces, boundary conditions, and convergence for each case.

Workspace. src/main.jl and src/collision/; Airfoil Lab also uses the pinned solvers/lbm_lbmtau/ copy.

3D geometry · GPU/AMRLUDWIG D3Q39 + entropicCompressible thermal lattice Boltzmann flowDevelopment · numerical review

Application. Research on three-dimensional compressible flow. The workbenches allow the lattice to be chosen explicitly; the main solver's automatic regime selection normally uses D3Q39 above Mach 0.6.

Mathematical method. A 39-velocity lattice Boltzmann population uses an entropic collision operator and a thermal distribution to evolve compressible density, velocity, and thermodynamic pressure. Multiple AMR levels are supported subject to domain and GPU memory checks.

Maturity. Development only. Short demonstrations and mesh paths exist, but general force and flow-field accuracy are not established. The October 2026 Mach 0.2 cube outputs were explicitly rejected after visible pressure artifacts; those outputs must not be used as validation or steady-state evidence.

Workspace. src/main.jl and src/collision/kernel_entropic_d3q39.jl; Airfoil Lab also has a pinned LBM snapshot.

These are visible here so their existence is clear. They are not enabled as workbench run options.

2D/3D compressible researchPonD finite-volume kinetic solverConservative moment fluxes; thermal, DUGKS, and AMR experimentsPrototype

Application. Compressible flow research on canonical shocks, cylinders, spheres, and airfoils.

Mathematical method. Particles-on-Demand kinetic equilibria supply conservative finite-volume face fluxes. Separate modules explore thermal transport, a DUGKS viscous flux, GPU kernels, and nested or block AMR. A single-level 3D case driver uses immersed-body volume penalization.

Maturity. Numerical unit and canonical-case tests exist. The source describes production-block AMR integration as further work; it is not a validated general body-flow backend.

Workspace. src/collision/pond/ and private tools/pond_*.jl experiments.

2D periodic referenceNative barotropic mixture coreConservative contact and acoustic verificationVerification only

Application. Controlled two-phase mixture, contact, and acoustic tests.

Mathematical method. A conservative finite-volume update evolves density, two momentum components, and vapour partial density on a uniform periodic grid. A barotropic equation of state supplies pressure and sound speed at Runge–Kutta stages.

Maturity. Restricted CPU reference with numerical checks. It lacks curved walls, open boundaries, turbulence, and the other pieces of a validated hydrofoil solver.

Workspace. src/barotropic/.

Air/water interface researchC-mesh free-surface coreThree-constituent interface and flux contractsExperimental core

Application. Resolved air/water interfaces, including overturned free surfaces, as a future underwater-flow path.

Mathematical method. Conservative constituent state, pressure-equilibrium closure, and candidate interface flux/projection operators are implemented in isolated modules.

Maturity. Its source explicitly says it is not admitted as an underwater-airfoil backend. Gravity balance, transport, and free-surface benchmarks must pass first.

Workspace. src/cmesh/cmesh_free_surface*.jl.

Mathematical method. A transported composition scalar with an algebraic mixture-pressure projection and segregated phase-change stepping.

Maturity. The source marks tem_step! legacy only; current cavitating runs use the conservative Species step. TEM configuration functions remain live and must not be confused with the old stepping path.

Legacy 3D latticeLUDWIG D3Q19Nineteen-velocity low-Mach lattice Boltzmann pathRetired

Former application. Lower-cost low-Mach 3D lattice Boltzmann cases.

Mathematical method. Nineteen discrete velocity populations with collision and streaming; legacy kernel and case code remain in the source and archive.

Maturity. Retired from the active regime selector and workbench choices. Revival requires a separate solver review, regression tests, and physical validation before it can be offered as a run option.

Workspace. Legacy D3Q19 files under src/ and the private archive.

Source snapshots and research modules can share numerical components without representing separate qualified products. Always inspect a case's mesh, residuals, physical balances, and comparison data before trusting its outputs.
