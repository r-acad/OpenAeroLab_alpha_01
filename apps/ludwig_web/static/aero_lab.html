Solver and mesh

Solver capabilities and maturity
D3Q27 + cumulantIncompressible regime, Mach at most 0.6.

These are development solvers; thermal kinetic FV is experimental. D3Q19 runs on the CPU octree solver, which needs no GPU. See the complete solver guide for capabilities and limitations.

CPU octree mesh

Cell-level octree: the finest level follows the surface to within two cells and no cells are stored inside the body. RAM, not VRAM; no GPU is used.

Flow and run

Thermal kinetic FV · experimental

Ideal air, γ = 1.4. Total energy is solved. No turbulence model; aircraft drag and stall predictions are not validated.

Read results

How to load and view results
  1. Saved analysis: File ? Open study, then click a recent study. Its saved fields attach automatically.
  2. Show fields: use Surface, Recorded planes, or Images & files above the canvas. Load saved results refreshes the linked analysis.
  3. Other files: choose a result folder or native / VTK files below. Selecting a file in Images & files also offers View.
  4. Planes: select a recorded plane and quantity, then wheel to zoom or drag to pan. Fit plane restores the full view.
  5. New plane positions: use saved volume VTK, or record those planes before another run. Moving a display plane does not record data.

Draft edits keep historical fields available. Their status identifies the saved run; create a new mesh to apply changed setup.

Read LBMP/LBMSLICE, inline VTU/VTP, or ASCII legacy VTK. Upload AMR metadata with native surfaces to recover placement.

      Reading the 3D view

      Domain axes stay fixed. Model axes follow the geometry rotation. Teal arrows show the prescribed inlet flow. Gold arrows show computed velocity from the recorded plane selected under Cut plane fields; their lengths are scaled for display. The i button lists inlet, outlet, and wall or far-field conditions; the domain faces are shaded by their role. Ground cases use solver Y as the vertical axis; other cases use Z.

      Use AMR levels to show each level and choose block outlines, cell outlines or translucent voxels. Large volume displays are sampled. The section uses actual cell sizes and omits coarse cells covered by finer levels. Drag the plane border or use the Sections slider; fields at arbitrary positions require a saved full-volume VTK snapshot. Inspect the cell size, wall coverage and memory summary before running.
